# Python 
Energy Consumption Analysis 
# Project Overview 
•The Energy Consumption Analysis System is a Python-based project developed to analyze and monitor daily energy usage in an industrial facility. 
•The system calculates energy consumption trends, evaluates whether energy usage exceeds predefined targets and safety limits, and determines if an engineering investigation is required. 
•The project demonstrates how Python fundamentals can be applied to solve a real-world engineering problem in energy management 
# Tools and Concepts Use
•Python
•Variables
•Data Types (String, Integer, Float, Boolean)
•Arithmetic Operators
•Assignment Operator
•Comparison Operators
•Logical Operators (and, or,not)
•Variable assignment 
# Features
•Stores facility and energy consumption data using meaningful variables.
•Calculates the increase in daily energy consumption.
•Computes the percentage increase in energy usage.
•Updates energy consumption after additional usage is recorded.
•Checks whether the daily energy target has been exceeded.
•Verifies if energy consumption remains within the maximum safe limit.
•Determines the operational status of the facility using logical conditions.
Identifies whether an engineering investigation is required.
Generates a clear and professional energy consumption analysis report.
# Outcome of Analysis
•Energy consumption increased by 1,300 kWh (10.40%).
•The facility exceeded its daily energy target but remained within the safe consumption limit.
•An engineering investigation is required to identify the cause of the increased energy usage.
# Recommendations
•Investigate the factors responsible for the rise in energy consumption.
•Implement energy-saving measures to reduce future usage.
•Continue monitoring energy consumption to ensure it stays within safe and target limits.
